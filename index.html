Reading the radar page

    Why a contact goes missing

    • Elevation. The beam is about 3° tall. Four bars cover roughly 9°. At 20 nm that is a slab about 20,000 ft thick; at 5 nm it is 5,000 ft.
    • Azimuth. Narrow the scan and it refreshes faster, but anything outside the wedge is invisible.
    • Size and range. A bomber shows at twice the range of a fighter. A cruise missile shows late.
    • The notch. A pulse-Doppler radar throws away returns that move like the ground. A target flying across your line of sight has almost no closure of its own and drops out.
    • You locked someone. In STT the antenna stares at one target. Everything else disappears.
